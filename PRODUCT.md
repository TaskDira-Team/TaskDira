# TaskDira
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Turn household chores into shared progress through quests, lifetime XP, spendable coins, levels and household-defined rewards. Keep the existing React, Vite, Three.js and .NET service contracts.

## Users
Families and roommates sharing household responsibilities. The selected aesthetic intentionally feels like a children's App Store game; this does not narrow the supported household audience.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Household membership and roles; assigned chores, completion and proof approval; saved tasks and alternate task views; earned lifetime XP and a separate spendable balance; household-defined rewards; milestones, rankings and avatars; English and Hebrew with RTL. Account actions and permissions remain real. The public miniature-house playground is illustrative and separate from account records. Analytics derive from available records and disclose missing history. Do not invent testimonials, adoption figures, activity or balances.

## Brand Commitments
The user replaced the restrained miniature-home direction with an emphatically childish kids' App Store game: shiny coins and XP, Duolingo-like winding quest roads, toy-shop reward shelves, colourful milestones and a friendly original companion. The palette is cream, teal, coral, lilac and yellow. The user dislikes boxed components; prefer open scenes and data compositions, with bounded controls and dialogs where needed. Preserve TaskDira's own identity instead of copying another product's mascot or artwork.

## Evidence on Hand
Frontend/src/routes.js, context/AppContext.jsx, services/, pages/ and components/. The locally generated 3D house and existing house illustration support the world. Frontend/public/images/dira-mascot.png is the original reusable companion. DESIGN.md records current visual authority from game-world.css, world.css, community.css, landingWorld.css and gameOnboarding.css.

## Product Principles
Make the next useful action obvious. Celebrate shared effort through real progress. Keep spendable coins distinct from lifetime XP. Use trails without inventing unlock constraints. Distinguish household data from examples. Preserve account and permission boundaries. Support touch, keyboard, visible focus, reduced motion and both reading directions. Delight must not hide labels, errors, balances or permissions.
